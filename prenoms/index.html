France in Numbers

Une question, des données, une réponse en français.

Opening

France publishes tens of thousands of public datasets on one site. Let’s ask France a question and answer it, en français, with real numbers.

Method

  1. Open Insee’s Classement des prénoms en France depuis 1900 (opens in a new tab) (the data also lives on data.gouv.fr (opens in a new tab)).
  2. Add a French first name to the graph and find the years it peaked.
  3. Write three sentences in French: the name, its peak, and a guess about why.
  4. Trade with a partner and check each other’s numbers.
Open the Insee tool (opens in a new tab)

Writing

Le prénom ______ était très populaire en ______.
