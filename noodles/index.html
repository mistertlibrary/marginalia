The Noodle and the Mosque

How can we chase a bowl of noodles across centuries of history?

Opening

Lanzhou beef noodles are pulled by hand, cooked halal, and sold in shops across China. They come from the Hui, Chinese Muslims of the northwest. Let’s slurp our way back in time!

Method

  1. Locate Lanzhou on a map that features the Silk Road.
  2. Read two accounts of the noodle’s origin. Where do they agree and disagree?
  3. Find out what makes the noodles chewy. Where did that ingredient come from?
  4. Write one paragraph: what does this bowl tell us about Islam in China?
