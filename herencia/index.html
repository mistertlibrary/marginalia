Herencia

Read old Spanish to help the Library of Congress. (Our nation’s libraries need all the help they can get!)

Opening

These documents were recorded in Spanish centuries ago. Today, you’ll set paper to pixels.

Method

  1. Open Herencia on By the People (opens in a new tab) and choose a page marked “Not started.”
  2. Open the Spanish cheat sheet beside it.
  3. Transcribe line by line, exactly as written. Keep old spellings. Type [?] for any word you cannot read.
  4. Save, then log three words that are rendered differently today.
