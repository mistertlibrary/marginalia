Fourteen Italies

One word across a hundred Italian villages.

Opening

In the early 1300s, Dante enumerated at least fourteen vernaculars in Italy. Between 1928 and 1940, two linguists published an atlas documenting how villages across Italy and southern Switzerland said everyday words. Let’s see who was “right” (if anyone).

Method

  1. Open NavigAIS and search for an everyday Italian word (perhaps botte).
  2. Find three different forms of the word and where each was recorded.
  3. Compare them with contemporary “standard” Italian (as taught at West Essex). Which region(s) sounds most like the textbook?
