Who Borrowed What?

A View of Paris, 1919 to 1941, one library card at a time.

Opening

Sylvia Beach kept a card for every member of her Paris lending library: what they borrowed, when, and where they lived. More than 600 of those cards survive. Let’s peer over a few shoulders...

Method

  1. Open the Shakespeare and Company Project (opens in a new tab) and select a member, famous or not. (Honestly, you might wind up with somebody famous whose name you just didn’t recognize.)
  2. List three books they borrowed and the dates.
  3. Look up one of those books. Why might this reader have wanted it then?
  4. Compare with a classmate’s notes. What sort of material was this portion of Parisians reading?
